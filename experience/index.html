Research experience

2023–present

Doctoral Research · Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision

Griffith University · Research in association with CSIRO

Conducting doctoral research on self-supervised learning for plant disease recognition, with emphasis on label-efficient representation learning and efficient computer vision architectures.

The research investigates Vision Mamba and state-space models, contrastive learning, teacher-student learning, multi-scale representation learning, vision-language modelling, lesion-context learning, and counterfactual reasoning for plant disease recognition.

Major research outputs include ConMamba, PSMamba, StateSpace-SSL and LeCo³.

2019–2021

Research · Intelligent Transportation & Lane Perception

Multimedia University, Malaysia

Conducted research on deep-learning approaches for lane-marking detection and road-scene perception, focusing on encoder-decoder neural networks, semantic segmentation and instance segmentation.

The research investigated robust lane detection under challenging road and environmental conditions, including curves, shadows, illumination variation, rain and low-light environments.

Thesis: Lane Markings Detection Using Encode-Decode Instance Segmentation Network

2013–2018

Research · Medical Image Analysis

Pabna University of Science & Technology, Bangladesh

Conducted undergraduate research on computer-aided medical image analysis, particularly automated detection of gastrointestinal bleeding from Wireless Capsule Endoscopy images.

The research involved image preprocessing, colour-space analysis, feature extraction, image-processing techniques and machine-learning-based classification for identifying bleeding regions in endoscopic imagery.

Thesis: Small Intestine Bleeding Detection Using Wireless Capsule Endoscopy
