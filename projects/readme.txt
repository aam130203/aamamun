              <p class="justified-text">
                This systematic review provides a comprehensive technical synthesis of self-supervised learning (SSL) for plant disease detection, addressing the challenge of limited large-scale annotated agricultural datasets. It systematically organises existing SSL approaches into generative, predictive, contrastive, and hybrid learning paradigms, and analyses their architectures, pretext tasks, datasets, evaluation strategies, and applicability to plant disease recognition. The study also identifies key methodological limitations and research gaps, providing a structured foundation for developing more effective and scalable SSL-based plant disease detection systems.
              </p>
