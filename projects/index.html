Plant Disease Detection Using Self-Supervised Learning

Label-efficient representation learning for plant disease recognition, including contrastive learning, Vision Mamba, state-space models and lesion-aware learning.

Featured manuscripts

2026 published Pattern Recognition

ConMamba

ConMamba is a self-supervised Vision Mamba framework for plant disease detection. Its main technical contribution is a Vision Mamba Encoder with a bidirectional State Space Model (SSM) for efficient long-range dependency modelling, combined with a dual-level contrastive learning objective with dynamic weight adjustment. This design enables adaptive learning of complementary local and global disease representations while reducing reliance on manually annotated data.

2026 published Expert Systems with Applications

PSMamba

PSMamba is a progressive self-supervised Vision Mamba framework for learning hierarchical and multi-scale plant disease representations. Its key technical contribution is a shared global teacher with two specialised students that progressively capture global structure, mid-scale lesion patterns, and fine-grained local symptoms. A hierarchical distillation mechanism and cross-scale consistency alignment are used to transfer and coordinate knowledge across different spatial scales, enabling more comprehensive disease representation learning.

Paper ↗
2026 published AgriAI 2026 (workshop) AAAI 2026

StateSpace-SSL

StateSpace-SSL is a linear-time self-supervised learning framework for plant disease detection built around a Vision Mamba state-space encoder. Its main technical contribution is the use of directional state-space scanning to model long-range lesion continuity across leaf surfaces without the quadratic attention cost of Vision Transformers. A prototype-driven teacher-student learning objective aligns representations across multiple augmented views, promoting stable and lesion-aware feature learning from unlabeled plant images.

Paper ↗
2026 submitted WACV 2027

LeCo³

LeCo³ is a lesion-context contrastive and counterfactual vision-language framework for evidence-aware plant disease recognition. Its main technical contribution is a two-stage architecture that first learns image-text representations from disease-name-free lesion and context descriptions, then performs soft semantic routing to separate lesion evidence from contextual support. A context-controlled fusion mechanism integrates these complementary cues, while alternative-aware counterfactual learning distinguishes visually similar diseases and supports comparative “why-not” explanations for plausible competing diagnoses.

02

Lane Marking Detection Using Deep Learning

Deep-learning approaches for road-scene perception, including encoder-decoder segmentation and systematic analysis of lane-marking detection methods.

Featured manuscripts

2021 published International Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering

SegNet

Lane Marking Detection using SegNet presents a lightweight encoder-decoder deep learning framework for semantic segmentation of road lane markings under challenging driving conditions. Its main technical contribution is a simplified SegNet-based architecture that combines convolutional encoding, max-pooling, deconvolution, up-sampling, batch normalization, and dropout to extract lane features while controlling computational complexity and overfitting. The model is designed to detect lane markings across varying road and environmental conditions, including rain, low light, cloudy scenes, straight roads, curves, and highly curved roads.

Paper ↗
2023 published Heliyon

EDIS-Net

EDIS-Net is a deep learning framework for robust lane-marking detection under diverse road and environmental conditions. Its main technical contribution is an encode-decode instant segmentation architecture based on E-Net, where the decoder is separated into binary segmentation and instance segmentation branches to learn both lane-pixel information and pixel-level lane associations. The framework further combines cross-entropy and discriminative losses and applies DBSCAN clustering to group predicted lane pixels into individual lane markings, enabling structured lane extraction across challenging scenarios such as shadows, illumination variation, obstacles, and curved roads.

Bleeding Detection

This work presents a computer-aided bleeding detection framework for Wireless Capsule Endoscopy (WCE) images. Its main technical contribution is a lightweight pipeline that combines targeted image preprocessing, RGB-to-HSV colour transformation, statistical feature extraction, and a Weighted K-Nearest Neighbour (WKNN) classifier to distinguish bleeding from non-bleeding frames. The method constructs a compact six-dimensional feature vector from the mean and local standard deviation of hue, saturation, and value, allowing bleeding-related colour characteristics to be captured with relatively low computational complexity. 10.1109ICASERT.2019.8934589 10.1109ICASERT.2019.8934589 The WKNN stage further assigns greater influence to nearer neighbours through distance-based weighting, making the classification more adaptive than standard KNN. The complete framework was designed to reduce the manual burden associated with reviewing large volumes of WCE imagery.

COVID-CXDNetV2

COVID-CXDNetV2 is a deep learning framework for automated detection of COVID-19 and pneumonia from chest X-ray images. Its main technical contribution is a modified YOLOv2 architecture integrated with residual learning, combining convolutional feature extraction with ResNet-style skip connections to support deeper and more stable representation learning. The architecture contains 14 convolutional blocks, five max-pooling layers, four residual connections, and dedicated classification layers, enabling both multiclass classification of COVID-19, pneumonia, and normal cases and binary COVID-19 detection. 26399-53467-1-PB 26399-53467-1-PB The study also constructed a customized chest X-ray dataset from four open-source repositories, with preprocessing and augmentation designed to support robust training across heterogeneous image sources.
