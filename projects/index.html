Research projects

My research is organised here by research program rather than by individual publication. Each project brings together the manuscripts and research outputs developed around a common scientific problem.

01
2023–present PhD research

Plant Disease Detection Using Self-Supervised Learning

This research investigates how computer vision systems can learn effective visual representations for plant disease recognition while reducing their dependence on extensively labelled training data. The work explores self-supervised representation learning, Vision Mamba and state-space modelling, multi-scale learning, and emerging vision-language approaches.

Self-supervised learning Computer vision Vision Mamba Representation learning Plant disease recognition
