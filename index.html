WELCOME

Research that learns from limited labels.

Artificial Intelligence · Computer Vision · Self-supervised Learning

I am a PhD candidate in the School of Information and Communication Technology at Griffith University, conducting research in association with CSIRO. My doctoral work investigates self-supervised learning for plant disease detection, including Vision Mamba and vision-language models.

My earlier research spans image segmentation, lane marking detection, medical image analysis, and renewable energy. Alongside my research, I contribute to higher education teaching and bring experience in academic leadership and program coordination.
